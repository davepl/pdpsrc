Timing notes:
- dry prints a rounded floating-point rate; dry29 truncates its integer
  rate and derives rounded microseconds from that rate.
- The default REG macro is empty and no -DREG flag was used. The sources'
  printed 'with register' banner is misleading; original output is retained.
- sieve uses gettimeofday(); krsieve sums per-pass whole-second time()
  differences. Krsieve's printed internal 30.00 seconds differed from real
  elapsed time, so it was not used to derive comparable throughput.
- A sieve run finishes its last complete pass, so actual duration can exceed
  the requested 30 seconds. Known limits were used to avoid 16-bit overflow
  and the sources' misleading validation of unknown limits.
