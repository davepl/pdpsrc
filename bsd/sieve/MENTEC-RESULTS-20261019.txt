Cause and implementation
------------------------
2.11BSD user-space long division uses floating-point instructions in ldiv.s.
The old sieve's long division/remainder therefore invoked FPSIM repeatedly.
32-bit addition, shifts, and integer printf conversion do not require FP.

The bitmap loops now use unsigned 16-bit byte/bit offsets, integer shifts,
and additions. Eight fixed-mask lanes mark odd composites without long
multiply, divide, or remainder in the loops. The maximum bitmap is 31250
bytes at limit 500000. Timing and decimal formatting use integer arithmetic
and shift/subtract division; average time is displayed in milliseconds.

Prime counts use long results (41538 exceeds signed 16-bit range). Limits
outside 2..500000 and durations outside 1..32767 are rejected. Allocation
failure is fatal; limits without a reference count print UNKNOWN.
